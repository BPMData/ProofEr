# Disaster Artist: checked figures

Stage: figures (pass 1 + pass 2 gap-fill, both done 2026-11-24). Every number, name and date the essay uses must have a row here.
Worker reports: `research/workers/pass1/g1-counts.md` (counts), `g2-money.md` (money), `g3-arles.md` (Arles),
`g4-afterlife.md` (afterlife and market), `g5-life.md` (life before Arles); `research/workers/pass2/gap-fill.md`.

**Status key.** `checked` = two independent sources, at least one primary or institutional, opened and confirmed.
`single source` = one good source only; usable only with a visible hedge if Bryan accepts it. `disputed` = good
sources disagree; show the range or both. `unverified` = not traced; stays off the page. `myth` = a popular claim the
research doesn't back; usable only as a myth-check (house taste T10, T23).

**Access limit.** vangoghletters.org and most vangoghmuseum.nl pages render only through JavaScript, so the workers
could open few letters directly; several letter quotes come from older translations (webexhibits.org, 1958
numbering, marked CL) and need a recheck against the 2009 edition before they are quoted on the page. Bryan can open
those pages in a browser; see "Open questions" at the end.
