# Round 2 research: verdicts

Bryan asked on 2026-11-24 for Claude to run the four prompts in `../gemini-prompts.md` itself, one research worker
each. These are the four reports. Wikipedia appears in them only as a lead, marked as such. vangoghletters.org and
the Pushkin's English collection pages refused automated reading, so some letter quotes and one museum page are
second-hand; each report says which.

| Topic | Report | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Gauguin in Arles | `gauguin.md` | "Unsalable" is not in any Gauguin letter found; only "incompatibility" of character is supported. Who paid his fare and Pont-Aven debts: not found (Theo's 150 F for a picture a month is confirmed). The portrait exchange came before he arrived. The razor story is only in his 1903 memoir. *Christ in the Garden of Olives* is a self-portrait, but only one magazine mentions the red hair; the red-haired Christ in the letters is Bernard's. Teha'amana was 13, Pau'ura 14. Syphilis assumed, not proven. |
| Johanna | `johanna.md` | No scholar found who says she "sanded the friction out" or made Theo a saint: the claim is untested, not supported. Documented changes are narrow (letter 416 / CL 347 printed without two enclosed sheets; "various errors and omissions"), and the 1914 text still prints Vincent's money complaints. Married Johan Cohen Gosschalk in August 1901 (announced 21 Aug); he died 18 May 1912; no later marriage. 39 of Theo's letters survive, first published 1932. "Her family urged her home": not found. |
| The chicken coop | `rey-chicken-coop.md` | Tradition, not documented fact, with no named first teller. The Pushkin's own page repeats it without a source ("first banished to the attic, later covered a hole in the chicken coop"). Rey's 1928 interview (Max Braumann, *Kunst und Künstler*) says it went to the garret and "it is said" replaced a broken windowpane: no coop. Safe wording: "A tradition repeated by the Pushkin Museum, without a source, says ...". |
| Three suspect claims | `three-claims.md` | All three false or garbled. No Boussod & Valadon show of his work in January 1890 (his real 1890 shows: Les XX, Brussels, 6 paintings; the Indépendants, Paris, 10). No Stedelijk or city purchase from 1905 (1905 was Johanna's exhibition; the state bought the collection in 1962). The Rey portrait entered the Pushkin in 1948, when the State Museum of New Western Art was dissolved, not 1960. |
