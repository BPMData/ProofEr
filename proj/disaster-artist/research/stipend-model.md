# Theo's ledger: the stipend model

No published year-by-year total of Theo's support was found. The primary record is Chris Stolwijk & Han Veenenbos,
*The Account Book of Theo van Gogh and Jo van Gogh-Bonger* (Van Gogh Museum / Primavera Pers, 2002), which the
research could not open. Until someone checks it, the ledger toy runs on this model and says so in its dialog.

## What the letters show (figures.md F20–F24)
- 1881–82: about 100 F a month, by Vincent's own reckoning of what he needed (letters 164, 201).
- 1884–85, Nuenen: 150 F a month (letter 440 and its note; letter 527).
- 1886–88, Paris: no cash allowance assumed; he lived in Theo's flat (to confirm, F23).
- 1888, Arles: about 250 F a month, Vincent's own figure (letter of c. 18 Aug 1888, CL 523).
- Cross-check: the Van Gogh Museum's catalogue essay (*Contemporaries of Van Gogh 1*) gives Theo's income as
  11,000–14,000 F a year and says 14.5% of it went to Vincent on average: about 1,600–2,000 F a year.

The ledger's end state should read "about 16,000 F (13,000–19,000)" rather than the brief's 18,000 F, which sits in
the range but has no source of its own. If Bryan prefers a round headline, "roughly 15,000 to 20,000 francs" covers
the model and the 14.5% cross-check.

## Conversions (hedged, F26–F28)
- 1890 gold parity: about 5.18 F to the US dollar (citation pending), so 400 F ≈ $77 and 16,000 F ≈ $3,100.
- Through US prices (×37 to 2026, a rough splice): 400 F ≈ $2,900; 16,000 F ≈ $115,000.
- Better frame for readers: a French coal miner earned about 1,060–1,080 F a year in 1880–88 (*Journal de la
  Société de statistique de Paris*, 1891). In the Arles year Theo's cash came to roughly 2.5 to 3 miners' wages.
