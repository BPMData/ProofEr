# Disaster Artist: research prompts (pass 1)

Bryan asked Claude to run the research itself (channel decision, Nov 22), so these prompts go to isolated
research workers rather than to the client. Each worker writes one report to
`proj/disaster-artist/research/workers/pass1/<group>.md`.

Rules shared by every prompt:
- For every figure give: value or range, unit, source title, author or institution, year, page/letter number/URL.
  Primary sources first (vangoghletters.org, Van Gogh Museum, museum collection pages, archives, catalogues
  raisonnés, peer-reviewed journals, auction-house records, major newspapers of record for sale reports).
- At least two independent sources per figure; where sources disagree, list each value with its source and why.
- Never cite Fandom, Kiddle, or similar aggregators. Wikipedia, Reddit and Medium are leads only: follow them to the
  original institution, book or journal and cite that.
- Never invent a number, date, quote or citation. Tag anything single-source or untraced `LOW CONFIDENCE`.
- If an anecdote can't be traced to a primary teller, say who tells it and that it is untraced.
- End with a table: ID · claim · value · sources · agreement · confidence · verdict on the brief's version
  (holds / adjust / wrong / can't verify).

## G1 · Counts (brief §6 rows: paintings, drawings, letters, per-year bars, Arles, Saint-Rémy, self-portraits)
Settle Van Gogh's output: total paintings (Van Gogh Museum figure, de la Faille 1970, Hulsker), drawings, surviving
letters (2009 edition counts: by Vincent, to Theo, to him), self-portraits. Then paintings per year 1881–1890, or
per period (Etten/The Hague/Drenthe/Nuenen, Antwerp, Paris, Arles, Saint-Rémy, Auvers) where per-year counts don't
exist in a source. The brief's per-year guesses (1881:1, 1882:30, 1883:80, 1884:100, 1885:120, 1886:60, 1887:75,
1888:200, 1889:140, 1890:65) are unsourced; replace them with sourced numbers or say they can't be sourced.

## G2 · Money (Theo's stipend, francs conversion, Goupil)
From the letters (vangoghletters.org), find what Theo sent Vincent and when: the monthly amounts by period
(1880–1890), the move to 150 F, then 250 F in Arles, paint and canvas paid separately, the Paris years when they
lived together. Find any published scholarly total of Theo's support. Build a year-by-year estimate with stated
assumptions and error bars. Also: a defensible 1890 franc → US dollar (gold parity) and → today method; what a
French schoolteacher or worker earned per year c. 1885–90; Theo's employer (Goupil & Cie → Boussod, Valadon & Cie
from 1884) and his role there; how Vincent left Goupil in 1876 and why.

## G3 · Arles (Gauguin, the ear, the petition, the Yellow House, the two self-portraits)
Gauguin's arrival and departure dates; who paid his fare and debts; what Gauguin wrote to Theo in December 1888
(the brief says he called the work "unsalable"; check the actual wording); the café argument; the ear (part or
whole, with the 2016 Bernadette Murphy / Dr Rey drawing evidence and the Van Gogh Museum's current position); who
received it ("Rachel" vs Gabrielle Berlatier); Gauguin's later razor account and why it is doubted. The petition:
date, number of signatories, what it asked for, where it is held, what the police/mayor did. Yellow House
destruction date. Vincent's letter describing the self-portrait as a bonze/Japanese monk (letter number, exact
words on colours); Gauguin's letter on "Les Misérables". Gauguin's later life: death date and place, and the
documented facts about his relationships with very young girls in Tahiti and the Marquesas (stated plainly, sourced).

## G4 · Afterlife and market (sales, shows, Johanna, auctions)
The Red Vineyard sale (buyer, price, date, venue) and any other documented sales or trades in his lifetime. Every
exhibition he was in during his life (Les XX 1890, Indépendants 1888–1890) and whether a Boussod & Valadon show of
~20 works in January 1890 existed. Aurier's article (title, journal, date). After death: Johanna's exhibitions and
loans 1892–1925, the 1905 Stedelijk show and whether the Stedelijk bought anything from 1905, Bernheim-Jeune 1901
(number of works), the 1914 letters edition and what she left out, whether she remarried, when Theo died and where
he was reburied. The Van Gogh Museum: founding foundation, opening date, the family collection's size. Auctions:
Irises (1987), the 1987 Sunflowers sale, Portrait of Dr Gachet (1990): house, date, price, buyer, what broke the
record and when, where Gachet is now. Dr Félix Rey portrait: the Pushkin Museum's own provenance and its telling of
the chicken-coop story, and when the Pushkin acquired it (the brief says 1960). Père Tanguy: Communard history
(arrest, sentence, deportation or not, release), his shop, and what became of the Van Goghs he held.

## G5 · Life before Arles (careers, beloveds, training, death)
Dated chronology: Goupil (hire date and age, The Hague, London, Paris, dismissal date and stated reasons), Ramsgate
and Isleworth, Dordrecht bookshop, Amsterdam theology study, Borinage (dates, why the post wasn't renewed). The
beloveds: the London landlady's daughter (Eugénie vs Ursula Loyer, who refused him), Kee Vos, Sien Hoornik (dates,
family veto, death in 1904 and how), Margot Begemann. Training: Anton Mauve, the Antwerp academy, Cormon's studio.
Japan: his print collection, the Japonaiserie copies, what the kanji in the Flowering Plum Tree border are and
whether he could read them, the Meiji-era export of Japanese art. The death: 27/29 July 1890 facts; Naifeh & Smith
2011 accident argument; the main scholarly pushback (Van Tilborgh & Meedendorp, Burlington Magazine 2013) and any
forensic commentary.
