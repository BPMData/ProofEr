## 1 · Gauguin in Arles → save as `gemini/gauguin.md`

```
I'm fact-checking a sourced data essay about Vincent van Gogh. I need the documented record of Paul Gauguin's stay
in Arles (23 October – c. 25 December 1888) and of his later life, at the level of letter numbers and exact wording.

Answer each question below. For every answer give: the claim, the exact wording where a letter or book is quoted
(original French or Dutch plus an English translation), the source (author or institution, title, year, letter number
in the 2009 edition at vangoghletters.org where one exists, page or URL), and how confident you are.

1. What did Gauguin write to Theo van Gogh in December 1888 about leaving? Give the letter (date, number in the 2009
   edition) and its exact words. We found "incompatibility" of their characters (c. 11–12 December). Does the word
   "unsalable" (or "invendable") appear in any letter of Gauguin's from Arles, to Theo, Émile Bernard or Émile
   Schuffenecker? If it does, quote it; if not, say so plainly.
2. Who paid Gauguin's train fare to Arles and his debts in Pont-Aven? What exactly was Theo's arrangement with him
   (amount per month, in exchange for what, from when)? Cite the letters.
3. The self-portrait exchange: when did Vincent paint "Self-Portrait Dedicated to Paul Gauguin" (Harvard Art Museums,
   Fogg) and when did Gauguin paint "Self-Portrait 'Les Misérables'" (Van Gogh Museum)? Were they exchanged before
   Gauguin arrived in Arles? Quote Vincent's description of his portrait as a "bonze" (letter number, exact words,
   including what he says about the colours) and Gauguin's description of his own (letter number, exact words).
4. The night of 23 December 1888: list every account by Gauguin of Vincent threatening him with a razor
   (Gauguin's letters of 1888–89, Émile Bernard's letter to Albert Aurier of 1 January 1889, Gauguin's "Avant et
   après", written 1903), with dates, and say how they differ. What do modern scholars (Van Gogh Museum, Martin Bailey,
   Bernadette Murphy, Hans Kaufmann and Rita Wildegans) say about the razor story's reliability?
5. Did Gauguin leave Arles on 24 or 25 December? Did he travel back with Theo?
6. "Christ in the Garden of Olives" (1889, Norton Museum of Art): is it documented as a self-portrait, and does any
   text source (Gauguin's letters, the Norton's catalogue entry, scholars) mention the red hair? Vincent's letters
   mention a red-haired Christ: whose painting is that about?
7. Gauguin's later life, stated plainly with sources: his "marriages" in Tahiti and the Marquesas to girls aged
   reported to be 13 or 14 (Teha'amana, Pahura, Vaeoho): their ages as documented and who documents them (his own "Noa Noa", letters, biographers
   such as David Sweetman, recent museum framing such as the National Gallery London's 2019 "Gauguin Portraits");
   his death (date, place); whether syphilis is proven or assumed.

Rules:
- Primary sources first: the letters (vangoghletters.org, the 2009 edition by Jansen, Luijten and Bakker), Gauguin's
  correspondence (Merlhès edition), museum collection pages and catalogues, peer-reviewed journals, books by named
  scholars.
- At least two independent sources per answer. Where sources disagree, give each version with its source.
- Never cite Fandom, Kiddle or similar aggregators. Wikipedia, Reddit and Medium are leads only: follow them to the
  original source and cite that.
- Never invent a quote, date, letter number or citation. If you can't find something, say "not found" and list where
  you looked.
- End with a table: question number · claim · value or wording · sources · do sources agree · confidence.
```

## 2 · Johanna van Gogh-Bonger: the 1914 letters edition and her second marriage → save as `gemini/johanna.md`

```
about money and Gauguin and turned Theo into a saintly caretaker. I need to know what is actually documented.

Part A: her editorial decisions in the 1914 edition
1. What did she leave out of the 1914 edition? List every documented omission or change: whole letters left out (by
```
