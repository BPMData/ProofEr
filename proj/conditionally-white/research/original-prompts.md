# IRISH ESSAY — DEEP RESEARCH PROMPTS

**Companion to `conditionally-white-brief.md` and `irish-essay-primary-source-pack.md`. Hand this file to the research orchestrator. Each batch below is a ready-to-run prompt for a sub-agent (or a deep-research pass). Batches are independent unless noted; run them in any order, but Batches Q1–Q6 must finish before any prose is written, and Q1–Q4 are the single highest priority (the quotes wall is the essay's credibility).**

**Global instructions for every sub-agent:**
- Prefer primary sources (parliamentary papers, the reports themselves, contemporary newspapers in digital archives, letters) over secondary citations. When a claim survives only through a secondary chain, record the full chain.
- Output format: a fact sheet per batch. Entries: `CLAIM → FINDING → SOURCE (title, author, date, URL/archive) → CONFIDENCE (verified / probable / unverified) → QUOTE-READY EXCERPT (exact wording, exact citation)`. If verification fails, say so plainly and list what the closest verifiable substitute is.
- Quote discipline: never "correct" a quote to sound better; transcribe exactly, and note edition/page/URL for every excerpt.
- Flags: the pack pre-flags two items (Duke of Cambridge quote = shaky; Sullivan & Hickel = contested). Treat pre-flagged items with extra skepticism, not less.
- Numbers: for every statistic, capture the original unit, scope, and period (e.g., "live animals, 1846–50, all Irish ports" vs "cattle only").
