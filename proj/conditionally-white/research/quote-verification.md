# Conditionally White: quote verification

Every quote the essay may use, traced to its primary source. **Rule: a quote ships only as VERIFIED (read in the
primary) or SECONDARY (read in a named scholarly source, with the chain shown in the footnote). UNVERIFIED and
UNVERIFIABLE quotes stay off the page**, and the Chapter 4 wall's caption notes each one that was evicted.
Exact wording below is as printed in the source named; where the pack's wording differs, the difference is noted.
Worker sheets with full citations are in `fact-sheets/` (Q-IDs).
