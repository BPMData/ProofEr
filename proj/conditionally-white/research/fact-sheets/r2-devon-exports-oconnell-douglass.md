## What this changes for the essay
1. **M3 can now be cited to the *Digest* itself**, with the wording above (p. 397 inferred; Table 95 on p. 564; its
   conclusion on p. 565 inferred). But the full sentence turns the meaning: the *Digest* pairs the 192,368 removals
   with **resettlement on about eight acres each of reclaimed Irish waste land**, says the scheme would "permanently
   provide" for them, and argues **against** shipping them to Canada. Presenting 192,368 as a clearance quota in the
   essay's own voice would cherry-pick. The honest framing: "Mitchel read a *Digest* table that counted 192,368 families
   to be moved off holdings under eight acres. He left out the same sentence's plan to settle them on reclaimed
   waste land, which was never carried out." (The last clause is a fact still to check.)
2. M6 citation fix: speech of 10 May 1854, printed in *Frederick Douglass' Paper* 19 May 1854. Douglass never names
   Mitchel.
