**Most important correction (O1):** the O'Connell line is not "come out of such a land, you who love liberty".
It is addressed to Irish Americans and ends in a threat to disown them: "Over the broad Atlantic I pour forth my voice,
saying, 'Come out of such a land, you Irishmen; or, if you remain, and dare countenance the system of slavery that is
supported there, we will recognize you as Irishmen no longer.'" It was said at the Loyal National Repeal Association,
Corn Exchange, Dublin, on **9 May 1843**, not in 1845.
