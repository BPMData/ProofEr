## Headline findings
- **ICJ dates, as the brief has them, are out of date.** Israel's Counter-Memorial deadline was extended **twice**: first to 12 Jan 2026 (Order of 14 Apr 2025), then to **12 Mar 2026** (Order of 20 Oct 2025). Israel filed on **12 Mar 2026**. An Order of **21 May 2026** fixed South Africa's **Reply for 22 Nov 2027** (the brief is right on this) and Israel's **Rejoinder for 22 May 2029**. Israel's Counter-Memorial includes objections to jurisdiction. There is no merits ruling, and none is possible before the written pleadings close in 2029.
- **The arms-sale notification threshold in the brief is wrong for Israel.** The $14m / $50m thresholds apply to most countries. Israel is in the NATO+ group, where the thresholds are **$25m (major defense equipment) / $100m (defense articles and services) / $300m (design and construction)** (CRS RL31675, 4 Jan 2024; The Guardian, 6 Mar 2024).
- **The WaPo story is confirmed:** John Hudson, published 6 Mar 2024 (reprinted in the Boston Globe on 7 Mar 2024). It reports "more than 100 separate foreign military sales", a figure that came from a classified briefing to Congress, not from a public document. Matthew Miller's defense: the administration "followed the procedures Congress itself has specified" and had "engaged Congress … more than 200 times".
- **Biden's "over the top" remark** was made on 8 Feb 2024 at a White House press conference called about the special counsel's report: "the conduct of the response in Gaza, in the Gaza Strip, has been over the top" (CFR, secondary). The official transcript was not reached. The White House said afterwards (Kirby, 12 Feb 2024) that this was not a new position.
- **The UN Commission of Inquiry's genocide finding is confirmed:** 16 Sep 2025. Navi Pillay: "The Commission finds that Israel is responsible for the commission of genocide in Gaza." Israel's Foreign Ministry: "Israel categorically rejects this distorted and false report and calls for the immediate abolition of this Commission of Inquiry."
- **The CBC split on the 1994 crime bill (my own tally):** on the conference report (House roll call 416, 21 Aug 1994, 235–195), **23 of the 37 Black House Democrats voted yea, 12 nay and 2 did not vote**. Gary Franks (R‑CT, Black) also voted yea. This is my own count against the Clerk's roll; the membership list comes from memory and still needs checking against a CBC roster.
- **The Rafah pause is confirmed:** a shipment of 1,800 2,000‑lb bombs and 1,700 500‑lb bombs (NPR, 8 May 2024). Biden on CNN: "Civilians have been killed in Gaza as a consequence of those bombs."
- **Not verified this session:** the 1994 Act's dollar provisions, Biden's 2019 "mistake" quotes, and the whole busing record (item 2). See the leads below.

### Q14B-1a — The 1994 Act: Biden's role and provisions

The 100‑to‑1 crack/powder disparity came from the 1986 Anti‑Drug Abuse Act (which Biden co-sponsored), not from 1994.

  - Senate adopted the conference report on 25 Aug 1994, 61–38.
  - Biden introduced the Senate bill (S. 1607, Nov 1993), which passed the Senate 95–4 on 19 Nov 1993.

### Q14B-3a — The WaPo report of 100+ below-threshold sales
- CLAIM: The Washington Post (6 Mar 2024, John Hudson) reported that the US approved and delivered more than 100 separate foreign military sales to Israel, each below the threshold for individual congressional notification. The brief gives the threshold as "$14m / $50m?".
- FINDING:
  - The report is confirmed: John Hudson's story was published Wednesday, 6 Mar 2024, and reprinted in the Boston Globe on 7 Mar 2024 under the headline "US floods arms into Israel despite growing alarm on war".
  - Its wording: the US "quietly approved and delivered more than 100 separate foreign military sales" since the war began, a figure officials gave members of Congress "in a recent classified briefing". Each sale "fell under a specific dollar amount that requires the executive branch to individually notify Congress."
  - The items included "thousands of precision-guided munitions, small diameter bombs, bunker busters, small arms and other lethal aid" (Semafor's summary of the WaPo report).
  - **Threshold correction:** Israel is among the countries (NATO, Japan, Australia, South Korea, Israel, New Zealand) with **$25m / $100m / $300m** thresholds and a 15-day review period. The $14m / $50m / $200m thresholds apply to other countries (CRS).
  - The count of "more than 100" exists only in the reporting of a classified briefing; no public document gives it.
  - Separately and publicly, the administration twice used AECA emergency authority in Dec 2023 to skip congressional review (CBS, CSM, NBC and PBS headlines). The amounts, about $106m in tank ammunition and $147.5m in 155mm components, come from headlines only; the articles were not read.
- SOURCES:
  - John Hudson, "US floods arms into Israel despite growing alarm on war", Boston Globe (reprinting the Washington Post), 7 Mar 2024, via PressReader: https://www.pressreader.com/usa/the-boston-globe/20240307/281621015297067
  - Julian Borger, "US uses loophole to keep 100 arms sales to Israel under the radar amid Gaza war – report", The Guardian, 6 Mar 2024, republished on Yahoo News: https://www.yahoo.com/news/us-uses-loophole-keep-100-192502838.html
  - Diego Mendoza, Semafor, 6 Mar 2024: https://www.semafor.com/article/03/06/2024/us-quietly-approves-more-than-100-sales-of-weapons-to-israel-report
  - Paul K. Kerr, "Arms Sales: Congressional Review Process", CRS RL31675 (v.68), 4 Jan 2024: https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2024-01-04_RL31675_c66c464fa3e80dbdc6d5a10237f4a512c3aea0bf.html
- STATUS: Report VERIFIED (date, byline, wording). Threshold VERIFIED, and CONTRADICTS the brief's $14m/$50m. The count of 100+ is SECONDARY (it rests on the reporting of a classified briefing).

### Q14B-4 — UN Commission of Inquiry: genocide finding, and Israel's rejection
- CLAIM: The UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel concluded in September 2025 that Israel has committed genocide in Gaza. Israel rejected the finding.
- FINDING:
  - **Date:** 16 Sep 2025 (OHCHR press release, reposted on UNISPAL).
  - **Conclusions:** the release states that "Israel has committed genocide against Palestinians in the Gaza Strip", and that "The Commission also finds that Israel has failed to prevent and punish the commission of genocide".
  - **Chair Navi Pillay:** "The Commission finds that Israel is responsible for the commission of genocide in Gaza" and "It is clear that there is an intent to destroy the Palestinians in Gaza".
  - **Acts found:** four of the five acts in the 1948 Genocide Convention: killing; causing serious bodily or mental harm; "deliberately inflicting conditions of life calculated to bring about the destruction of the Palestinians in whole or in part"; and imposing measures intended to prevent births.
  - **Incitement:** the Commission found that Herzog, Netanyahu and then-Defence Minister Gallant incited genocide.
  - **Report symbol:** A/HRC/60/CRP.3, a conference room paper. Known from a UNISPAL document URL and title only; the report itself was not read.
  - **Israel's response:** a statement on 16 Sep 2025 (posted on the website of Israel's mission in Geneva, carried by UNISPAL) said: "Israel categorically rejects the libelous rant"; "The current COI report relies entirely on Hamas falsehoods". The Foreign Ministry posted on X: "Israel categorically rejects this distorted and false report and calls for the immediate abolition of this Commission of Inquiry", and called the members "Hamas proxies, notorious for their openly antisemitic position" (Times of Israel). Ambassador Danny Meron called the findings "cherry-picked" (UN News).
- SOURCES:
