**The route that worked this pass: archive.org's full-text search API.** It searches inside every OCR'd item and returns
short snippets (about 10 words either side of the hit). By chaining exact phrases ("A … B", then "B … C") the full
sentence can be read in the primary scan, even though the scan's text file is far past WebFetch's 106k-character cap.
- The address form is `https://archive.org/services/search/beta/page_production/?service_backend=fts&user_query=<"phrase one" "phrase two">&hits_per_page=N`.
  The quoted phrases are ANDed.
- Its `page_num` field is the item's leaf count, not the page of the hit (246 for op1246629-1001, 630 for ER vol. 87),
  so **this route gives wording but no printed page numbers.**
- Snippets are raw OCR. I give the wording with obvious OCR slips fixed ("arc" = are, "Enc^lish" = English, "depdts" =
  depôts). Each fix is checked against a second scan where one exists.
