## Corrections to the pack
1. **The "[...]" before "She died within five days" hides that the old woman was carried out alive and put in a
   shed; MacLeod says she died five days later.** A caption implying she burned to death misreads him.
2. **MacLeod gives the line two ways:** Letter IV (1840–41, reprinted 1892 p. 9): "Damn her, the old witch, she has
   lived too long; let her burn." The 1857 Stowe rejoinder (1892 p. 90): "D—n her, she has lived too long, let her
   burn." Cite the letter, and frame it as MacLeod's allegation against a man a jury acquitted.
3. **"Patrick Sellar was subsequently tried for culpable homicide; he was acquitted" is not MacLeod's sentence.**
   MacLeod says he was tried before Lord Pitmilly for "culpable homicide, fire-raising, &c." and "acquitted".
   "John Mackay" is printed "John M'Kay". The letters were first collected in **1841** (Edinburgh), not 1987.
