**Method.** curl to archive.org failed (no connection), so everything went through WebFetch. Two routes worked:
(1) archive.org full-text search across all scanned books,
`https://archive.org/services/search/beta/page_production/?service_backend=fts&user_query=<phrase>&hits_per_page=N`.
It returns highlight snippets **even for lending-restricted books** (Prebble, Grimble, Richards 2000), which is how
the Prebble wording below was read; (2) in-book search (`inside.php`) on unrestricted items. The `inside.php`
"page" values are **scan leaf numbers**, not printed pages. For the 1892 MacLeod they were mapped to printed pages
with the item's `page_numbers.json`; for Thomas Sellar 1883 and Blackie 1885 they were not mapped (marked "leaf").
The fetch tool caps each quote at about 125 characters, so long passages were read in consecutive pieces; they are
joined below only where the pieces were consecutive. About 50 page reads were used.

But Sellar's party came round and set fire to our house at both ends, reducing to ashes whatever remained within the walls. ## Corrections for F93 / PS-08 / PS-11 (for the lead to apply; this worker edits no other file)

1. Betsy MacKay: cite *The Celtic Magazine* IX no. 98 (Dec 1883) pp. 62–63, statement dated 29 Aug 1883, taken
   down by Angus Mackay for John Mackay of Hereford; not Prebble. She was 86 then and 16 at the burning.
2. Prebble's wording ("the burning party", "deserved no better,") is not hers as first printed.
3. Trial: Tuesday 23 April 1816, Circuit Court of Justiciary, Inverness, before Lord Pitmilly; charged with culpable
   homicide, real injury and oppression; unanimous Not Guilty after about a quarter of an hour; the judge said he
   agreed with the verdict. Sources: the 1816 trial report as reprinted by Thomas Sellar (1883); *Inverness Journal*
   26 April 1816 (extract in the same book); Richards (1971).
4. Heath burning: 1892 ed. pp. 7–8; "crop", singular; restore "as they had already lost their cattle,".
