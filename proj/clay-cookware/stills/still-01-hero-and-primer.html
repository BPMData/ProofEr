Glazed, Unglazed, and Mostly Undisclosed

Buying a clay pot—or an electric multi-cooker with a clay insert—means wading through romantic claims about purple silt, volcanic minerals, and "far-infrared" magic while the box hides the only three numbers that govern how the pot cooks: porosity, vitrification, and thermal expansion.

The Five-Second Takeaway Glazed or unglazed, a clay pot's trade name matters far less than whether its pores are open to water and oil, how low its thermal expansion runs, and whether the heat source warms it gradually enough not to snap it in half.

Visual Anchor · Cross-Sections (F01, F02, F12, F24, F25)

Unglazed Terracotta

e.g., Römertopf · 900–1,063°C
Water abs: 8–25% · Open pores: 15–35%

Saturates during a 15-min soak; releases steam to buffer food near 100°C (212°F) until dry.

Unglazed Zisha Stoneware

e.g., VitaClay · 1,100–1,220°C
Water abs: 2–6% · GB/T 10816 ≤6%

Semi-vitrified: holds liquid water without soaking, yet still adsorbs fatty acids & aromas.

Burnished Micaceous Clay

e.g., La Chamba · 750–800°C
Porosity: unmeasured (analog 21–26%)

Aligned clay platelets shine like glaze; platy mica deflects flame cracks before they split the pot.

Glazed Kaolin Stoneware

e.g., Magnifique · Vitrified Glass
Vitreous body: 0.5–3% · Porcelain ≤0.5%

Blocks flavor & soap entry; traps food moisture on smooth silica, where wet proteins can stick.

How to decode a pot when the box won't tell you its firing cone or porosity

Consumer Proxy Decoder (F11, F19, F25, F26, F29, F30)
Printed Care Clue What It Quietly Confesses Likely Ceramic Class Cooking Consequence
"Soak pot 15 min; cold oven only" High Open Porosity (8–25%) Low firing leaves continuous capillaries and high thermal-shock fragility. Unglazed terracotta / red earthenware (Römertopf) Self-basting steam dome; shatters if placed wet into a preheated oven or onto a burner.
"Never soak exterior; no oven/stovetop" Semi-Vitrified, Non-Flameware Moderate CTE + quartz phase; relies on electric jacket's gradual PID ramp. Unglazed Zisha stoneware (VitaClay) Liquid-tight without soaking; must brown meat in a separate skillet; still adsorbs oils/soap.
"Gas flame OK; use diffuser on electric" Crack-Deflecting Temper / Low CTE Either platy mica + low firing, or petalite/spodumene additives. La Chamba or LAS/MAS flameware (Emile Henry Flame) Survives open flame on low-to-medium gradual heat; electric coils create lethal hotspot gradients.
"Glazed interior; sear & 500–750°F oven" Glass-Sealed + Shock-Tuned Body Zero surface porosity; stovetop rating implies low-CTE body or stress-relief glaze. Flame-rated glazed kaolin / stoneware (Magnifique) Zero flavor memory, dishwasher/soap safe; still non-magnetic (0 induction coupling).
