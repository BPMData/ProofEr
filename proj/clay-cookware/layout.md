# Layout plan: Glazed vs. Unglazed Clay Cookware

Stage: layout · APPROVED 2026-11-20 by Bryan Murphy

Format: Responsive scrolling interactive data essay (desktop-first ~1040px test-kitchen notebook grid, single-column 390px phone layout) · The one sentence: _Glazed or unglazed, a clay pot's name matters far less than whether its pores are open to water and oil, how low its thermal expansion runs, and whether the stove or multi-cooker heats it gently enough not to crack it._ · The visual idea: **The Pot Wall Cross-Section & Test-Kitchen Lab Notebook** — treating every cookware material (soaked porous terracotta, dual-pore Zisha, burnished micaceous La Chamba, kaolin-glazed stoneware, petalite flameware, and cast iron/aluminum) as a physical barrier whose pore geometry, thermal inertia, and failure thresholds govern everything that happens to heat, water, oil, and food.
