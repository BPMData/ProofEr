- **Myths checked:**
  - Myth/oversimplification: 'Vedem was printed or distributed in multiple copies' — FALSE; only a single master copy of each issue was produced (first ~30 typed, remaining ~53 handwritten) and read aloud secretly on Friday evenings.
  - Myth/oversimplification: 'Out of 15,000 Terezín children vs. ~100 boys in Room 1, exact survivor count is universally 15' — NUANCED; authoritative survivor/archival sources disagree slightly between 'about 10 out of ~100–110' (Centropa) and 'roughly 15 out of ~100' (Paměť národa), while secondary summaries often cite '15 out of 92 Vedem contributors'.
