# Layout plan: `terezin-vedem`

Stage: layout · APPROVED 2026-11-22 by Bryan Murphy (dark-slate mode matching `:root[data-theme="dark"]` of `nazi-victims-all-groups.html` & `holocaust-by-country.html`)

Format: Responsive scrolling interactive data essay (`1000px` max reading width; single-column phone scroll; dark-slate default with clean light `@media print` stylesheet).
Palette & Type: Dark slate ground (`#15171A`), elevated slate surface (`#1C1F23`), warm stone ink (`#E7E4DD`), muted (`#A2A6AD`), hairline rule (`#34383E`), deep rose-crimson (`#B5475A`, `#FFF3F4` on filled bars), weathered stone (`#5B5750`), slate blue (`#79AFCF`), archival ochre (`#D4A650`); embedded `Spectral` (400/500/600) + `Public Sans` (400/500/600/700) via `tools/embed_fonts.py`.
The one sentence: *Behind a staged "model town" shown to inspectors in a four-to-eight-hour tour and eleven days of forced filming, Terezín crammed nearly sixty thousand people into space for seven thousand and sent tens of thousands—including almost every child—east to be murdered, while its young prisoners answered with four thousand drawings and eight hundred clandestine magazine pages.*
The visual idea: **Monument Unit Grids + Slide-Over Archival Dossiers** — interactive unit square walls, borderless comparison rows, and `dialog#sheet` archival panels pairing the staged outside glance against primary transport ledgers, licensed historical photographs/drawings, *I Never Saw Another Butterfly* (full poem + Czech original with attribution), and *Vedem* (Pepek's full editorial with attribution).
