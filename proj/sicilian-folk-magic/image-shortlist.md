# Images to send (for the puppet cutouts)

This environment can't download from museum image servers, so the paintings have to come from Bryan. Save each as a
JPG or PNG (the largest the museum offers) and attach them to a message. All are public domain or CC0 per
`research/F-images.md` (row numbers in brackets), where each license page is listed.

1. **Rosalia:** van Dyck, *Saint Rosalie Interceding for the Plague-stricken of Palermo*, 1624, the Met [#1]
2. **Rosalia's float:** Houël, *The car of Saint Rosalia*, aquatint, 1780s, Wellcome Collection [#7]
3. **Agatha:** *Saint Agatha*, bronze statuette, mid-17th c., the Met [#14]
4. **Agatha's bust (stand-in):** *Reliquary Bust of Saint Juliana*, circle of Giovanni di Bartolo, c. 1376, the Met [#62]
5. **Lucy:** Zurbarán, *Saint Lucy*, c. 1625/30, National Gallery of Art [#23]
6. **Joseph:** *St. Joseph*, Neapolitan crèche figure, 18th c., the Met [#30]
7. **Goddess:** two terracotta busts (Persephone or Demeter), Greek Sicilian, 4th c. BCE, the Met [#35]
8. **Isis:** *Statuette of Isis nursing Horus*, Late Period, the Met [#46]
9. **Tiles:** *Albarello*, Lazzaro workshop, Palermo, 16th c., the Met [#49]
10. **Frame:** *Gilt ripple frame*, Sicilian(?), late 17th c., the Met [#57]
11. **Ex-voto:** *A man praying to Sansovino's Virgin and Child*, Wellcome Collection [#71]
12. **Angels for the curtain call:** *Angel*, Neapolitan crèche figure, the Met [#64]

No open-licensed picture of Benedict the Moor or Antonio of Noto turned up; they stay drawn unless Bryan finds one.
