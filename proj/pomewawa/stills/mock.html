Plate I · Aztec roots

On the menu
since 1520.

  • from 1100 BCEMaya dogs at Ceibal, Guatemala, ate so much corn it shows in their bones. Some were brought in alive from the highlands.
    Sharpe et al., PNAS, 2018
  • 1520Cortés writes home about the great market of Tenochtitlan, where “little dogs” are raised for eating.
    Cortés, Second Letter to Charles V, Oct 30, 1520
  • TodaySome Chihuahuas still carry DNA on their mother's side from ancient Mexican dogs.
    van Asch et al., Proc. R. Soc. B, 2013
