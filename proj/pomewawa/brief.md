# Brief: The Pomewawa

Status: APPROVED Oct 26, 2026 by Bryan Murphy · Stage: research · Mode: from scratch · Client: Bryan Murphy · Sign-off: Bryan
Usage: not recorded yet (ASK: share of a 5-hour limit per stage, as the Claude app shows it)

## 1. The ask
An infographic about Pomeranians and Chihuahuas, and the Pomeranian × Chihuahua mix, which Bryan calls the
**Pomewawa** (never "Pom-Chi": "such a better portmanteau than pom-chi"). For dog lovers online and Bryan's family
"who love and hate our pomewawa". Shared online. Deadline: "now".

## 2. Purpose and tone
- **Feel:** "Wow, I bet a Pomewawa could kick my ass in a fight."
- **Know:** tiny dogs, big-dog ancestry, a long strange history, and whatever they're measurably good at beyond
  "looking like an IRL tamagotchi" (research decides). (DRAFT)
- **Do:** laugh and share the link. (DRAFT)
- **Tone:** funny, bragging, affectionate (DRAFT, from the first messages).
- **Must not feel like:** the "dogs are better than people" crowd. "I love my dog but ... if a building was burning
  I'd pick like, most children over the dog." The brag is about the dog, never against people.
- **Framing (agreed):** the page can't claim the data proves they're "better than all other breeds". It shows what
  makes them stand out, with proof, and admits limits: "they couldn't beat a cane corso in a fight, we should mention
  that so maybe they're not the best."

## 3. How it's read
Mostly on phones (confirmed), shared online and in family chats, read alone. Format (scroll vs. tap-through):
**Claude's call** ("You decide that bro").

## 4. The subject
Bryan's pitch: "aztec ancestry + food for the winter + space-heater if the harvest was good = AWESOME!" Every part of
that is a claim for research (see §5), not a fact yet.
- **Never:** a "better than every breed" claim the data can't back; dogs-over-people sentiment; the family dog's
  name or photo ("don't dox my pomewawa").
- **Allowed and wanted:** ribbing owners who pick a breed for the wrong life ("people who own kangals but don't live
  on a farm or have livestock to protect"). The joke is on the choice, never on a named person. Origin: a Kangal
  breeder at the AKC Meet the Breeds event told Bryan "he didn't think so many people should own kangals" ("okay
  well you would know lamo"). Usable as Bryan's own anecdote, breeder unnamed; his words, not a sourced fact.
- **Dog as food:** "if people really did eat chihuahuas like a chicken wing you can mention it + other documented
  times dogs were used as food could be a fun drill-down menu, i.e. switzerland." Only documented cases, each
  sourced. **Angle (Bryan):** not "haha korean people eat dogs"; "if anything it should be more like 'actually
  everyone would eat a dog like a cinnabon if they had to'". **Landmine:** every culture gets the same plain, factual treatment. No "ew, *those* people" framing,
  which is how this topic usually turns into a dig at Asian countries. The Swiss can be teased; nobody is
  dehumanized.

## 5. The questions the numbers must answer
| # | Question | Why it matters to the reader |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Where do Chihuahuas come from? Is the Aztec / pre-Columbian ancestry (e.g. the Techichi) supported, and how sure are researchers (DNA vs. legend)? | "Aztec ancestry" is the hook |
| Q2 | Were these dogs eaten, and kept as bed-warmers ("space heater"), and by whom, when? What's documented vs. folklore? | "Food for the winter + space-heater" |
| Q3 | Where do Pomeranians come from (the big sled/Spitz ancestors, the size shrink, royal owners)? | Tiny dog, big-dog ancestry: the "kick your ass" angle |
| Q4 | What are the two breeds measurably good at or known for: lifespan, size vs. attitude, alertness/watchdog behavior, popularity, anything else research finds? | "I'm not sure what they're actually good at" |
| Q5 | How do they compare with a big dog like the Cane Corso (weight, bite force, etc.), honestly? | The joke that they'd lose the fight |
| Q7 | Documented times and places dogs were eaten (incl. Switzerland and pre-Columbian Mexico): who, when, how common, and is it legal or practiced today? | The food drill-down menu |
| Q8 | What were Kangals (and similar livestock guardians) bred for, and how big are they? | The "wrong owner" joke needs its facts right |
| Q6 | What's known about the Pomeranian × Chihuahua mix itself (size range, temperament), given mixes aren't standardized? | It's the family's dog |
